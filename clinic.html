04 / PARENT RESOURCES

Helpful answers,
without the jargon.

An accessible resource structure that the practice can edit and expand in WordPress.

How do I know where to start?+

Start by telling the practice what you have noticed and what questions you have. The team can help explain its process and whether a consultation or evaluation is appropriate. This is concept copy, not clinical advice.

What is an evaluation?+

An evaluation is a way to understand a child’s current communication or related skills and discuss possible next steps. The exact process depends on the practice and should be confirmed with its team.

Can I learn more about a service first?+

Yes. Select one of the three service cards above to see a page-ready overview and a short set of common questions for that service.
